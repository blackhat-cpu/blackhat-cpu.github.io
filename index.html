01 — About

I'm a Technical Lead & Developer working across Automation, Digitalization and IT‑OT Integration to build connected manufacturing ecosystems. Over 5+ years in industrial automation, I've moved from PLC programming, process logic development, SCADA design and VFD integration into Industry 4.0–driven digitalization — integrating PLC/SCADA with KEPServer, Node‑RED, SQL databases, REST APIs, MQTT and OPC‑UA to enable secure, real‑time data flow between plant operations and enterprise systems.

I work closely with cross‑functional teams across operations, IT and management to deliver scalable, impactful industrial solutions — including SAP–PLC recipe integration via REST APIs and real‑time data pipelines built with SQL, Power BI and Python, aimed at improving equipment effectiveness, reducing downtime and lifting process yield. I also design relational database schemas to log production data and process parameters for efficient storage, retrieval and trend analysis of high‑velocity time‑series data.
